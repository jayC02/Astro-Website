About Scotland EPC

Domestic EPCs and legionella risk assessments, done properly and priced clearly, from a Glasgow team that works in building energy every day.

Who we are

Scotland EPC is the domestic service of Scottish Energy Services, an energy and building compliance consultancy based at 272 Bath Street in Glasgow city centre. Scottish Energy Services produces SAP and SBEM calculations, commercial EPCs and other compliance work for developers, architects and businesses. Scotland EPC brings the same standards to homes, landlords and letting agents.

How we work

  • Accredited assessors. Every EPC is produced by an accredited domestic energy assessor and lodged on the Scottish EPC Register.
  • Clear prices. Starting prices by property type, plus VAT, confirmed before you book, with a single travel supplement outside G and EH postcodes.
  • One visit for both. EPC and legionella risk assessment together, so your tenant or agent only gives access once.
  • Quick paperwork. EPCs are normally lodged within 24 hours of the visit.
  • Straight answers. If you have a question about your rating or the actions in a legionella assessment, call us.
