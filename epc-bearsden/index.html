EPC Bearsden

Domestic EPCs for Bearsden and Milngavie homes, from sandstone villas to interwar bungalows and family houses.

Updated 18 November 2026 by the Scotland EPC team at Scottish Energy Services

Homes in Bearsden

Bearsden is mostly houses rather than flats, and many are larger or older than average:

  • Victorian and Edwardian villas with solid stone walls, bay windows and rooms in the roof. Extensions built at different times are common, so the assessor records each part separately.
  • Interwar bungalows and semis, often with cavity walls. Filled cavities and good loft insulation usually lift these to C or D.
  • Post-war and newer detached houses, where the boiler and heating controls make the biggest difference.

Larger homes with several extensions take a little longer to survey. Having the dates of extensions and any insulation paperwork to hand helps the rating reflect the work you have done.

What happens on the visit

An accredited assessor surveys every room, the boiler, the hot water and the loft if it can be reached safely, taking photos and measurements. Most visits take under an hour. Your EPC is lodged on the Scottish EPC Register and emailed to you, normally within 24 hours.

Questions

Do you cover Milngavie too?

Yes, along with the rest of East Dunbartonshire and the whole of Scotland.

My house has a loft conversion. Does that matter?

Yes. A room in the roof is assessed differently from a loft, so tell us when you book and have any building warrant or insulation details to hand.
