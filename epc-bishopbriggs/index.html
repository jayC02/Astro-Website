EPC Bishopbriggs

Domestic EPCs for Bishopbriggs, Torrance and Kirkintilloch, with no travel charge in G64 and G66 postcodes.

Homes in Bishopbriggs

Bishopbriggs grew quickly from the 1930s onwards, so most homes are semis, bungalows and terraces from the middle of the twentieth century, with newer estates on the edges:

  • 1930s to 1970s semis and bungalows usually have cavity walls. Whether the cavity has been filled, and how thick the loft insulation is, decide a lot of the rating.
  • Newer estates built since the 1990s are often timber frame with insulation built in, and usually rate C or better.
  • Flats around the town centre and Kirkintilloch, where the heating system is the main factor.

If you have had cavity wall insulation installed, a guarantee from the installer is the best evidence, because the assessor cannot always confirm it by looking at the outside of the wall.

Bishopbriggs EPC prices

Standard prices apply in G64 postcodes: from £70 for a flat, £75 for a maisonette, £80 for a bungalow and £85 for a house. A legionella risk assessment starts from £65, or £120 with an EPC. All prices plus VAT.

What happens on the visit

An accredited assessor surveys every room, the boiler, the hot water and the loft if it can be reached safely, taking photos and measurements. Most visits take under an hour. Your EPC is lodged on the Scottish EPC Register and emailed to you, normally within 24 hours.

Questions

Is there a travel charge for Bishopbriggs?

No. G64 and G66 are Glasgow postcodes, so standard prices apply.

How do I show my cavity walls are filled?

A guarantee or invoice from the installer is best. The assessor may also be able to see drill marks in the outside walls.
