EPC East Kilbride

Domestic EPCs across East Kilbride, Scotland's first new town, from 1950s terraces to newer estates.

Updated 18 November 2026 by the Scotland EPC team at Scottish Energy Services

Homes in East Kilbride

East Kilbride was designated Scotland's first new town in 1947, and most of its homes were built in planned phases from the 1950s onwards:

  • 1950s to 1970s terraces, semis and flats built for the new town. Some use non-traditional construction methods, which the assessor identifies and records because they affect how the walls are treated.
  • Former council homes that have since been improved with external wall insulation, new windows and modern boilers. Paperwork for this work helps the rating.
  • Newer private estates on the edges of town, usually rating B or C.

If you let several properties in the town, we can book them back to back on the same day.

East Kilbride EPC prices

EPCs in East Kilbride start from £70 for a flat, £75 for a maisonette, £80 for a bungalow and £85 for a house. A legionella risk assessment starts from £65, or £120 with an EPC. All prices plus VAT, and we confirm yours before booking.

What happens on the visit

An accredited assessor surveys every room, the boiler, the hot water and the loft if it can be reached safely, taking photos and measurements. Most visits take under an hour. Your EPC is lodged on the Scottish EPC Register and emailed to you, normally within 24 hours.

Questions

How much is an EPC in East Kilbride?

Flats start from £70 and houses from £85, plus VAT.

My house has external wall insulation. Will that be picked up?

The assessor will see it on the walls. Any guarantee or details of the scheme that installed it help record the right thickness.
