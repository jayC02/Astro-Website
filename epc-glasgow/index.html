Homes in Glasgow

Glasgow's housing is dominated by flats. Red and blond sandstone tenements fill the West End, Partick, Dennistoun, Shawlands and Strathbungo, alongside interwar and post-war homes in the outer neighbourhoods and newer flats along the Clyde. Each brings its own EPC issues:

  • Sandstone tenements have thick solid walls with no cavity to fill, so the rating depends heavily on the heating, the windows and whether the flat is on a middle floor, the top floor or the ground floor.
  • Top-floor flats lose heat through the roof. If there is a loft above, insulation there can make a real difference, but it is often shared, so evidence of any work matters.
  • Flats with electric heating, common in 1960s and 1970s blocks and some conversions, often rate E or lower. High heat retention storage heaters are the usual recommendation.
  • Newer flats and houses built since around 2010 usually rate B or C.

What happens on the visit

An accredited assessor surveys every room, the boiler, the hot water and the loft if it can be reached safely, taking photos and measurements. Most visits take under an hour. Your EPC is lodged on the Scottish EPC Register and emailed to you, normally within 24 hours.

Questions

How much is an EPC in Glasgow?

Flats start from £70 and houses from £85, plus VAT. We confirm your price before booking.

Can you get into a tenement close?

Yes, as long as someone can let us in at the door entry or the tenant buzzes us up. We only need to see inside the flat being assessed, plus the loft if it belongs to that flat.
