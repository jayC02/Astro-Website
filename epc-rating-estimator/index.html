    How the estimate works

    The estimator starts from a typical rating for a Scottish home of that age, then adjusts it for the type of home, the heating, insulation, windows and solar panels. The figures are based on how these features usually affect a domestic EPC. They are deliberately simple.

    This is a rough guide, not an EPC. A real rating is calculated by an accredited assessor from measurements of the home, its exact heating system and evidence of insulation, and is the only rating you can use to sell or let. Results can differ by a band or more.

    What moves a rating most

    Heating

    The main heating system has the biggest single effect. Replacing an old non-condensing gas boiler or direct electric heating usually moves a home up at least one band.

    Walls

    Filling empty cavity walls is cheap and effective. Solid sandstone and brick walls can be insulated internally or externally, but the work is more involved, especially in tenements and conservation areas.

    Loft

    Topping loft insulation up to 270mm is one of the cheapest improvements for houses and top-floor flats with their own loft.

    Renewables

    Solar PV panels and heat pumps lift both the current and potential ratings. They are recorded on the EPC with their size.

    EPC bands explained

    EPC energy efficiency bands and SAP points
    BandSAP pointsWhat it usually means
    A92–100Very efficient. Usually new homes with heat pumps or solar.
    B81–91Typical of homes built since about 2010.
    C69–80Good. A well-insulated older home with an efficient boiler.
    D55–68The most common band for Scottish homes.
    E39–54Poor insulation or older heating.
    F21–38Often electric heating with little insulation.
    G1–20Very inefficient and expensive to heat.

    The Scottish Government has proposed that private rented homes should reach Heat Retention Rating band C under the new-style EPC, which measures insulation rather than running costs. It is not the same scale as today's rating. Our 2028 guide explains the difference.
