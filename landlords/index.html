The Repairing Standard

Every privately let home in Scotland must meet the Repairing Standard at the start of a tenancy and throughout it. The Scottish Government's statutory guidance, updated from 1 March 2024, sets out what landlords should do for each part of it, including water safety, electrical safety, heating and alarms. Tenants can apply to the First-tier Tribunal if they think a home falls short.
