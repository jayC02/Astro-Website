Legionella risk assessment Edinburgh

Legionella risk assessments for rented homes and short-term lets across Edinburgh. From £65 per property in EH postcodes, or £120 with an EPC, plus VAT.

Short-term lets and HMOs

Edinburgh has a large number of short-term lets and HMOs. Short-term let hosts must meet the Repairing Standard, which includes the duty to assess legionella risk. Properties that sit empty between guests are a particular risk, because water stagnates in the pipes and shower hoses.

HMOs and larger flats often have shared tanks or long pipe runs. We will quote for these once we know the size of the system.

Older tenements

New Town and Marchmont flats often have cylinders in cupboards and, on top floors, tanks in the roof space. Old pipework that once fed a removed bathroom or kitchen can leave dead legs, which the Repairing Standard guidance says should be removed.

What we check

We identify the water system, measure temperatures at the taps, cylinder and any tank, inspect tanks for lids and debris, and look for showers, dead legs and little-used outlets. You get a written assessment with the temperatures recorded, an overall risk level and a short list of actions. More about legionella risk assessments.

The rules in Scotland

The Repairing Standard guidance says a private landlord must have the water system assessed for legionella before the property is first let, and review it at intervals of no more than two years. There is no legionella certificate and water testing is not normally needed.

Questions

Do short-term lets in Edinburgh need a legionella risk assessment?

Yes. Short-term lets must meet the Repairing Standard, which includes assessing legionella risk before letting and reviewing it at least every two years.

Can you do the EPC at the same time?

Yes. Both together cost £120 + VAT in EH postcodes.
