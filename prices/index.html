Price questions

Is there a cheaper EPC for a studio flat?

No. Flat EPCs start from £70 + VAT. The survey still has to cover the heating, hot water, walls, windows and lighting, however small the home.

What counts as a house?

Any terraced, semi-detached or detached house over more than one floor. Single-storey homes are priced as bungalows, and flats with their own front door over two floors as maisonettes.

Is a large house more expensive?

The house price covers typical family homes. If a home is unusually large or complicated, we confirm the price with you before booking.

Why is the bundle cheaper?

Both checks happen at the same visit, so your tenant only gives access once and we pass the saving on.
