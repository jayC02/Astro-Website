Privacy notice

This notice explains how Scotland EPC, part of Scottish Energy Services, 272 Bath Street, Glasgow G2 4JR, uses your personal information.

What we collect

When you ask for a price or book a visit, we collect your name, phone number, email address, the property address or postcode, and anything you tell us in your message. To produce an EPC we also record details and photos of the property.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, confirm the price and arrange the visit.
  • To produce your EPC or legionella risk assessment and send it to you.
  • To lodge the EPC on the Scottish EPC Register, as the law requires. The register is public and shows the property address and its energy data, but not your name or contact details.
  • To keep records we need for accounting and to meet our assessor accreditation requirements.

Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing that contract, and meeting our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to provide the service: with our accreditation body and the Scottish EPC Register for EPCs, and with the email provider that sends messages from our website form. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. Records of completed work are kept for as long as we need them for accounting and accreditation, normally up to six years.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The price calculator and checklist work entirely in your browser and do not store or send your answers.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Call 07483 881889 or write to us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
